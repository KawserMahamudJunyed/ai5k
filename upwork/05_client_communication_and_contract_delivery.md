# 05. Client Communication System & Upwork Contract Delivery Model

## 1. 20 Policy-Compliant Client Communication Templates

Upwork strictly enforces pre-contract communication rules. **All communications prior to a contract being officially hired MUST occur on Upwork Messages or via the native Upwork Zoom integration.** External contact details (email, WhatsApp, phone, external Calendly links) before contract start violate Upwork Terms of Service and result in account suspension.

### Template 1: Initial Response to Client Interview Invite
```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for inviting me to interview for your [Job Title] project! 

I've reviewed your job post and technical requirements. With over 30 years of technology leadership and hands-on AI cloud architecture experience, I am confident I can engineer a secure, production-ready solution for your team.

I am available for a brief video chat via Upwork Zoom to discuss your project scope. Please let me know what times work best for you, or feel free to suggest a time directly through the Upwork scheduling tool!

Best regards,
Alex | Enterprise AI Architect
```

### Template 2: Pre-Contract Zoom Call Confirmation (Policy Compliant)
```text
Hi [Client Name],

I look forward to our upcoming call! Per Upwork policies, our meeting will be hosted directly through the native Upwork Zoom link attached to this message thread. 

To help make our call as productive as possible, I will prepare a brief technical overview based on your requirements. See you on Upwork Zoom!
```

### Template 3: Explaining Hourly Work Diary Tracking
```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for hiring me on an hourly contract! To ensure total transparency and protect your project, I utilize the official Upwork Desktop App Work Diary to log active development time. 

Every hour billed includes detailed work memos and automated screen activity verification. You can review active work logs anytime via your client contract dashboard.

I am initiating our build sprint today!
```

## 2. Upwork Contract Delivery Operating Model

```
[Contract Hired / Escrow Funded] ──> [Kickoff & Access Assessment] ──> [Milestone / Hourly Sprint]
                                                                                │
[Client Handover & Video Demo] <─── [QA & Security Review] <─── [Code Engineering & Logging]
               │
               ▼
[Escrow Release / Hourly Sign-off] ──> [Public & Private Feedback] ──> [Retainer / Contract Expansion]
```

### Hourly vs. Fixed-Price Execution Rules

#### Hourly Contract Delivery SOP
- Always use the **Upwork Desktop App Work Diary** with clear, descriptive activity memos (e.g., `Building Pinecone vector index script in Python`).
- Upwork Payment Protection applies ONLY to hours logged via the Desktop App with active keystrokes and memo notes (Manual time is NOT protected by Upwork).

#### Fixed-Price Contract Delivery SOP
- Work begins ONLY when the contract is officially accepted and Upwork Escrow shows **"Funds Escrowed"**.
- Always submit work formally through the **"Submit Work for Approval"** button on the contract page to trigger the 14-day client review window.
