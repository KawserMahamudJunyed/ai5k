# 07. Reusable AI Skill: Upwork AI Profile, Proposal & Project Optimizer

> **Skill Name**: `upwork_ai_optimizer`  
> **Version**: 1.0.0  
> **Purpose**: Automated profile auditing, job post analysis, tailored proposal generation, Connects ROI calculation, and JSS tracking.

---

## 1. Skill Overview & Trigger Commands

The `upwork_ai_optimizer` skill functions as an AI copilot for Upwork freelancers and agencies. It parses client job posts, matches job requirements against specialized profile assets, drafts 3-line opening hooks, and enforces Upwork TOS policy compliance.

### Recognized User Triggers
- `"Draft an Upwork proposal for this RAG job posting."`
- `"Audit my Specialized Profile for AI Agent development."`
- `"Calculate if I should boost my proposal on this $5,000 job post."`
- `"Diagnose why my proposal view rate is below 15%."`
- `"Generate an Upwork Consultation agenda for an enterprise client."`

## 3. Skill Guardrails & Strict Constraints

> [!CAUTION]
> **Enforced Guardrail Rules**:
> 1. NEVER include external contact details (email, phone, WhatsApp, external links) prior to contract hire.
> 2. NEVER send automated proposals directly via API without human seller review.
> 3. NEVER recommend bidding on jobs with unverified payment methods and low client feedback ratings (<4.0 stars).
> 4. NEVER guarantee JSS scores or contract awards.
