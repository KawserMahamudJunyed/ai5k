# 03. Upwork Project Catalog Architecture & Paid Consultation Offerings

## 1. Upwork Project Catalog Overview

Upwork Project Catalog allows clients to purchase productized, fixed-price services directly from your profile without posting a traditional job description. This creates a high-margin inbound sales channel that operates 24/7.

## 3. Paid Upwork Consultation Offerings

Upwork Consultations allow clients to book paid strategy sessions directly on your calendar.

### Consultation Offer 1: 30-Minute AI Architecture & Strategy Sprint
- **Price**: **$150**
- **Target Buyer**: Startup Founders, Non-Technical Entrepreneurs, Product Managers.
- **Description**: A focused 30-minute video strategy call to evaluate your AI product idea, validate technical feasibility, select the right AI models/stack (OpenAI vs. Claude vs. AWS Bedrock), and establish realistic budget/timeline expectations.
- **Deliverables**: 
  1. 30-minute recorded video consultation call.
  2. 1-page Technical Recommendation Summary & Architecture Sketch.
  3. $150 credit applied toward any follow-on development contract.

### Consultation Offer 2: 60-Minute Enterprise AI Deep Scoping & Roadmap Call
- **Price**: **$250**
- **Target Buyer**: Enterprise CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Corporate Innovation Leads.
- **Description**: An intensive 60-minute deep-dive consultation covering enterprise RAG architecture design, multi-agent workflow orchestration, SOC2/HIPAA data security guardrails, vector database selection, and token cost optimization.
- **Deliverables**:
  1. 60-minute recorded technical strategy session.
  2. Formal Architecture & Security Scoping Roadmap (PDF).
  3. Recommended Technical Requirements Document (TRD) outline.
  4. Fixed-price implementation proposal for full project build.
