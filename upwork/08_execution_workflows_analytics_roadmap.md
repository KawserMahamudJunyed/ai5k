# 08. Operating SOPs, Analytics Dashboard & 365-Day Implementation Roadmap

## 1. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Quarterly Operating SOPs

### Daily Operating SOP (Time Required: 45 Mins/Day)
- **Primary Goal**: Scan job feeds, submit 2-4 highly targeted proposals, respond instantly to client invites, and log active contract hours.
- **Workflow Steps**:
  1. Review automated Upwork RSS job feed for target keywords (`RAG`, `LangGraph`, `AWS Bedrock`, `n8n`).
  2. Filter posts: Payment Verified, Hire Rate > 60%, Budget > $1,000 or > $80/hr.
  3. Generate tailored proposals using the `upwork_ai_optimizer` skill; customize 3-line hooks.
  4. Submit proposals and selectively boost Top 3 slots on high-budget enterprise posts.
  5. Respond to client interview invites within < 15 minutes.
  6. Log active development hours using Upwork Desktop App with clear memo descriptions.

### Weekly Operating SOP (Time Required: 2 Hours/Week)
- **Workflow Steps**:
  1. Review Job Success Score (JSS) updates on Sunday.
  2. Evaluate weekly proposal performance: Proposal-to-Interview Conversion Rate (Target: >15%).
  3. Track Connects expenditure vs. realized contract earnings (Target Connects ROI: >10x).
  4. Audit active hourly contracts; verify Work Diary memos and screen logs.
  5. Publish or update 1 Upwork Project Catalog item or Consultation availability slot.

### Monthly Operating SOP (Time Required: 4 Hours/Month)
- **Workflow Steps**:
  1. Conduct full profile review across General Profile and 2 Specialized Profiles.
  2. Evaluate realized hourly rate across contracts (Target: Incremental increase toward $150–$250/hr).
  3. Audit 30-day client private feedback trends.
  4. Perform 1 proposal opening hook A/B test.

### Quarterly Operating SOP (Time Required: 1 Full Day / Quarter)
- **Workflow Steps**:
  1. Assess progress toward **Top Rated Plus** badge ($10,000+ single enterprise contract within 12 months).
  2. Evaluate **Upwork Enterprise Talent Cloud** status and submit updated agency credentials.
  3. Review client retainer renewal rates (Target: >30% contract extension rate).

## 2. Upwork Analytics & 25 KPI Dashboard Framework

| KPI Name | Category | Definition / Formula | Target Threshold | Warning Threshold | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Job Success Score (JSS)** | Performance | Upwork Composite Performance Metric | **> 95%** | < 90% | Audit private client feedback & contract outcomes |
| **Proposal Win Rate** | Conversion | $(\text{Hired Contracts} / \text{Proposals Submitted}) \times 100$ | **> 12%** | < 5% | Improve 3-line opening hooks & portfolio proof |
| **Interview Conversion Rate** | Sales | $(\text{Interviews} / \text{Proposals Submitted}) \times 100$ | **> 20%** | < 8% | Target higher-fit jobs; boost selectively |
| **Connects ROI** | Efficiency | $\text{Gross Revenue Earned} / \text{Connects Dollar Cost}$ | **> 15x** | < 5x | Stop bidding on high-proposal, low-fit posts |
| **Realized Hourly Rate** | Revenue | $\text{Total Hourly Earnings} / \text{Total Hours Logged}$ | **>$120 / hr** | <$75 / hr | Increase quote rates on new proposal submissions |
| **Top Rated Plus Progress** | Status | Value of largest single contract in last 12 months | **>$10,000** | <$5,000 | Focus proposals on high-budget enterprise RFPs |
