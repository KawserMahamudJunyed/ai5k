## 5. 12 Myths vs. Verified Evidence in Upwork Optimization

| Myth | Status | Empirical Evidence / Official Fact |
|---|---|---|
| *"You can send external Zoom links to clients before a contract is hired."* | **REJECTED** | Upwork strictly prohibits off-platform communication prior to contract hire. Violations result in account suspension. |
| *"Boosting proposals with 50+ Connects guarantees you will get hired."* | **REJECTED** | Boosting places your proposal in the top 3 slots, but if your 3-line opening hook or profile fit is poor, clients will archive it instantly. |
| *"Manual time logged on hourly contracts is protected by Upwork."* | **REJECTED** | Upwork Payment Protection applies ONLY to time logged via the official Desktop App Work Diary with active keystrokes and memos. |
| *"Public 5-star ratings are all that matter for your Job Success Score."* | **REJECTED** | Upwork JSS is heavily weighted by **private client feedback**. A client leaving 5 stars publicly but 6/10 privately will drop your JSS. |
